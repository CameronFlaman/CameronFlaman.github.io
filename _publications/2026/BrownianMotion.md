abstract: >-
  Brownian motion is a quintessential example of a stochastic process, which often serves as a foundational intro- duction to the subject of statistical physics for students. This project aims to create a physical setup that allows for the observation of Brownian motion on a macroscopic scale. The setup applied an actuating, square frame powered by a DC motor; collisions with the frame imparted momentum to a collection of steel bearing balls cre- ating visible stochastic motion of a larger 3D printed puck. The motion of the puck was tracked and statistical tests were performed, comparing the observed motion of the puck to the theoretical probability distributions of the Brownian motion model. While the setup serves as a strong demonstration of a stochastic process and qualitatively exhibits behavior similar to Brownian motion, its actual motion was found to be much more complex — subject to a combination of super and sub-diffusive processes.
